#+TITLE: topology
#+SUBTITLE: Draw what you have, from the files that describe it
#+LANGUAGE: en
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil
#+STARTUP: showall

Point topology at the git repositories you keep.  Each can say what it is in
a short =TOPOLOGY.md=; topology reads them, sorts what they say by where,
what, how and who, and draws it with TopoForge.  You move the shapes; the
drawing is yours.

The browser app works with no server at all ("Zero Sharing": everything
stays in your browser).  With a server, every feature can be switched off,
down to just the app's files on disk.

* The drawing app: topology forge (formerly TopoForge)

The drawing app in =www/topoforge/= is *topology forge (formerly TopoForge)*.  Bryant
Frankford made it, for Corwin; he is named first, here and on everything that grows from it.  Its
work goes on in this branch (Corwin, 2026-11-01): what was the TopoForge project is folded in here,
and =topoforge/= carries its kits, specs and recipe.

The naming rule: in our own text (READMEs, NEWS, ChangeLog, documents, the app's About box) say
"topology forge (formerly TopoForge)".  Do not rename what saved drawings and pages that embed the
app rely on: =window.TopoForge=, the TopoForge JSON format, the =topoforge.*= storage keys, file
names, and the brand and title in the app, which a visitor opening it on its own sees.  From 1.8 a
page may embed it with switches (no frame, floating panels, page tabs in the overview map, a
selection hook, a ready event): =topoforge/spec/EMBED-v1.md=.

* The painting app: topology paint

=paint/= holds *topology paint*, made for Bryant Frankford: a drawing and painting program in C for
Windows, Linux and macOS, with pen pressure and tilt (written with the XP-Pen Artist 13.3 Pro V2 in
mind), brushes, layers with blend modes, OpenRaster and Photoshop files with their layers, the
common flat formats, and mods.  =paint/build.bat= (Windows) or =paint/build.sh= builds, installs and
updates it; =sh bin/test.sh= checks its core with any C compiler.  [[file:paint/README.md][paint/README.md]] says the rest,
with [[file:paint/docs/MODDING.md][how to mod it]] and [[file:paint/docs/LESSONS-LEARNED.md][what was learned]].

* Try it

- No install: open =www/index.html= after =sh bin/stage-www.sh /tmp/site=,
  or serve =/tmp/site= with any web server.
- Packages: =sh bin/fetch-sources.sh && dpkg-buildpackage -us -uc=, then
  =apt install= the three =.deb= files (or only =topology-www=).
- Check a copy: =sh bin/test.sh=.

* Colours: egad

The pages use the egad design system's colours and type by default (black on
white or white on black, text at 7:1 or better).  The app's settings offer
"the first palette" and a light or dark choice; both are kept in this browser
only.  =www/css/egad.css= is copied from egad's =dist/css/egad-tokens.css=
and says so; =tests/a11y-check.mjs= measures every pair the stylesheet draws.

* Accessibility

=node tests/a11y-check.mjs= checks the two pages against the WCAG 2.1 AA
rules we promise (language, one h1, heading order, landmarks, skip links,
labels, names, keyboard reach, contrast in five palettes);
=node tests/a11y-check.mjs --plant= breaks a copy thirteen ways and each must
be caught.  =sh bin/test.sh= runs both.  It does not replace trying the app
with a screen reader and a keyboard; please report what it misses.

* Examples

=examples/inputloop/= describes inputloop's own projects as =TOPOLOGY.md= files,
one folder each.  Three are private and say =private: hide=; what they hold is
under =## Private=.  After changing one, run =node tests/make-examples-fixture.mjs=
and read the diff; Perl and JavaScript must both still agree with it.

* Reading the Perl

The data modules use perl's new =class= syntax; the parts an operator reads
are classic Perl.  =perl/Topology/doc/CLASSIC-BRIDGE.pod= maps one onto the
other.  The tests run under perl 5.44.0 (core =class=) and perl 5.38
(Object::Pad, through Feature::Compat::Class).
