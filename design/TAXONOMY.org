#+TITLE: Topology's taxonomy: four pillars, two axes, six tensions
#+SUBTITLE: The prose reading of taxonomy/pillars.json
#+LANGUAGE: en
#+STARTUP: showall
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

This taxonomy stands alone: you need nothing from C4, inputloop or anyone's
notes to use it.  It was grown from them (the sources are named at the end),
and it is kept as data, in =taxonomy/pillars.json=, which the server
(=Topology::Taxonomy=) and the browser app (=topology-core.js=) both read.
Change a word there and both change.  The tests fail if the app shows a word
that has no row (=perl/Topology/t/40-taxonomy.t=).

* The four pillars

Every thing a drawing holds answers one question first.  The pillar is that
question.

| pillar | asks                                       | holds                                                        |
|--------+--------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Place  | Where is it?                               | hosts, sites, racks, folders, repositories as places, mirrors |
| Part   | What is it, and what is it made of?        | components, packages, modules, versions, containment          |
| Path   | How does it connect, flow or happen?       | links, transports, processes (BPMN, BPMN+), flowcharts, logic |
| Person | Who may see it, change it, or answer for it? | people, roles, consent, privacy, credentials, the hand on go |

A thing can be read in more than one pillar.  A repository is a place you can
clone and a part you can package.  The taxonomy does not force one reading;
it records the first (=pillar=) and any others (=also=), and the tensions
below say what each pair of readings pulls against.

Why these four.  They are the four questions a person drawing a system asks
of every shape, in the order they usually ask them: where, what, how, who.
They map onto TopoForge's own shape families without remainder (each of its
device, container and basic shapes sits in one pillar; the test checks the
shape names against the copy we ship), and onto the drawing tasks the prompt
names: physical layout (place), components and packages (part), BPMN and
flowcharts and logic (path), and private parts, credentials and "go" (person).

Falsifier: a shape or word that people keep placing in two pillars with equal
conviction.  That is a fifth question, or a tension that should be a pillar.

* Two axes that cross every pillar

** Layer: at what level is this drawn?

- *abstract*: an idea or a design; nothing need exist
- *operational*: how it runs: processes, services, schedules
- *physical*: what you could touch or ping

A =TOPOLOGY.md= may say =layer:=; the app can show one layer, or all of them
stacked.

** Act: what does drawing it do?

- *describe*: shapes or words describe something buildable
- *propose*: drawn over an existing thing, it is a proposed change, or asks for a prompt to make it
- *effect*: press go, and tools that can make the change make it

This axis is Bridget's, from the recording of 2026-10-31 (00:01:51 to
00:02:28): "if I'm putting shapes on the page or if I'm typing. Either way I'm
describing something I want to have be buildable ... If that's an existing
thing I'm proposing a change to it asking for a prompt to do it or literally
affecting that change if I press go and I have tools that can implement the
change for me."  In the app it is the row of three buttons over the drawing.

* Six tensions

Four pillars make six pairs.  Each pair pulls in a known way; the app names
the pull where it bears (in the upgrade wizard, beside a change; in the
graph, beside a thing read in two pillars) instead of listing all six
everywhere.

| between        | name                               | what to do about it                                              |
|----------------+------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------|
| place, path    | where it sits / how it moves       | draw once, show both readings; a mirror is both                   |
| part, person   | what it is / who answers for it    | separate rows that link: the package, and its maintainer          |
| place, person  | where it is / who may see that     | show-private applies to places too                               |
| part, path     | what it is / what it does          | the layer axis says which reading is drawn                        |
| place, part    | the box / what is in the box       | containment: a repository is both                                 |
| path, person   | the flow / the hand on it          | a BPMN lane is a person, its task a path; go only a person takes  |

* Words

The app's words, each with its pillar and its sense, are the =words= rows of
=pillars.json=.  A word the app shows that has no row is a gap: not an error,
a word to define.  The list is short on purpose; it grows when a word is
needed, never in advance.
