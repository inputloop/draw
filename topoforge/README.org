#+TITLE: topology forge (formerly TopoForge)
#+SUBTITLE: A single-file, offline network-topology designer
#+AUTHOR: Bryant Frankford and Corwin Brust
#+LANGUAGE: en
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil
#+STARTUP: showall

TopoForge draws networks: devices (routers, firewalls, switches, servers,
desktops and some thirty more), groups that hold them (site, zone, VLAN,
subnet, DMZ, VPC, rack; groups can sit inside groups), and connections glued
to the devices' sides, across pages and layers.  It checks the drawing as you
go (bad or duplicate addresses, an address outside its subnet, VLAN
mismatches, loose connection ends) and lists what it finds.

It is one HTML file, =index.html=.  It needs no server, no install and no
network: everything runs in your browser, and your work is saved in that
browser as you go.

TopoForge was created by Bryant Frankford, written with Alfred (for Bryant;
claude-opus-5-5), for Corwin Brust, who gave inputs to the prompt.

The name: from 2026-11-01 the project is *topology forge (formerly TopoForge)*, part of

Say "topology forge (formerly TopoForge)" in our own text (READMEs, NEWS, ChangeLog, documents,
the app's About box).  Do not rename what saved drawings and embedding pages rely on:
=window.TopoForge=, the TopoForge JSON format, storage keys (=topoforge.*=), file names, and the
brand and title a visitor sees in the app itself.

* Open it
Open =index.html= in a web browser: double-click it, or drag it onto a browser
window.  It works from =file://=, offline.  Use File › Export to keep copies
of your work outside the browser.

* What it reads and writes
- Saves and opens its own format, TopoForge JSON (=.json=).
- Exports SVG, PNG, PDF (vector, by way of print), CSV (with a template),
  draw.io (=.drawio=), Visio 2003-2010 XML (=.vdx=) and Visio (=.vsdx=).
- Imports its JSON, CSV, draw.io and Visio files.
- Visio export has been tried lightly by hand in Visio 2010 on Corwin's
  machine (avalon); this is reported, not yet a scripted test.
- New in 1.6: a live story of the drawing, in plain sentences, that can be
  downloaded as Markdown for a person or an AI to read beside the picture;
  and a lasting handle on each shape (=tf_ref=, written into its data as you
  draw), so a sentence can always be traced to its shape.
- New in 1.7: a page on the same origin can open a drawing with
  =?load=<path>= (a CSV or TopoForge JSON); any other origin, and =file://=, is
  refused and said; =window.TopoForge.importCSV= for the kit.
- New in 1.8: switches a page that embeds the app may turn on, all off when it
  is opened on its own (=?embed=frameless,props,shapes,pages,select,ready= or
  =TopoForge.embed({...})=): no frame, Properties and Shapes as floating
  panels, the page tabs folded into the overview map, a selection hook and a
  ready event (to a parent page of the same origin only), and every menu
  command by id (=TopoForge.commands()=, =TopoForge.run(id)=); each piece of
  the chrome can be hidden (=hide-menus=, =hide-tools=, =hide-shapes=,
  =hide-props=, =hide-pages=, or =bare= for all), and =TopoForge.shapes()= and
  =TopoForge.place(id)= let the page offer the shapes itself.  =spec/EMBED-v1.md=
  says it all; =tests/browser/embed.browser.mjs= checks it.
- New in 1.9: more for an embedding page, each off until asked for and none
  turned on by =all= or =bare=: the view (=TopoForge.view=, =setView=, and a
  message when it changes), where the selection is on screen, =place(null)=,
  =setProps(id, patch)= (one undo step), keys and right clicks for the page,
  a message when pages change, the overview map moved and resized by handles
  outside its frame, and a preview in it.  Everywhere: page tabs that do not
  fit show the ones that do, with ‹, › and a list of all of them.
- New in 1.10: the overview map's modes for a preview (=mapMode=: cards, both,
  rings), a card in the map, the matches lit in the drawing, the map's own
  scale (=mapScale=), and the wheel over the map (=mapwheel=).
- New in 1.11: the map's pad (=mappad=: a drawn slider for the mode, an arm
  for the scale, a grip; =padArrows('pan')= to pan instead) and Reset layout
  (=reset=, =resetLayout()=, =undoReset()=).
- New in 1.12: the card that fills the map (=bigcard=: its words as large as
  fit, large art or a picture of the page's own origin, a box score or lines,
  several matches as pages, =cardPage()=) and the pad that pages it
  (=cardpad=: it turns 45 degrees and changes colour while a card shows).
- New in 1.13: a peek at a placed shape's card (=peek=: hold Shift with the
  pointer over a shape, or press Shift alone with one shape selected, and the
  map shows its card until Shift is let go) and =fitScale()=, the zoom Fit
  would set, read without moving the view.
- New in 1.14: =peekKey(down)=, so a page whose own keys hear Shift can show
  the peek (the shape under the pointer's last place, or the one selected
  shape), and a big card's title too long to fit is shortened at a word with an
  ellipsis, not cut off.

* Accessibility
We want TopoForge usable without a mouse, without colour, and without seeing
the picture.  What is true of version 1.6, checked on 2026-10-08 in Chromium
with the browser kit (=tests/browser/=), and what is not yet:
- *Keyboard.* Tab reaches every panel; the arrow keys move between the
  Properties, Layers, Issues and Story tabs; Enter and Space work on them and
  on Download; the menu buttons open with Enter and close with Escape; the
  focus ring shows; and there is no place Tab gets stuck.  The menus'
  commands also have keyboard shortcuts (open, save, print, undo and redo,
  cut, copy, paste, duplicate, delete, select all, edit text with F2, front
  and back, group and ungroup, layout, zoom and fit).  Not checked yet:
  drawing and moving shapes on the canvas with the keyboard alone.
- *Labels.* Every field in the Properties panel is tied to its visible
  words, and an automatic scan (axe-core) finds no serious or critical
  problem in the light or the dark theme.  Headings go in order: the page
  heading is level one, the panel headings level two, the story's
  sections level three.  The scan finds nothing at any level.
- *A text alternative to the picture.* The story panel says in words what
  the drawing shows: which devices there are, which group holds which, what
  connects to what, and what the drawing does not say.  Changing text is
  announced to screen readers.
- *Not tried yet:* a real screen reader (NVDA, Narrator, Orca) and browsers
  on Windows.
- *Colour.* Meaning should never be carried by colour alone; where it is
  today, that is a bug to report.
- *Plain words.* Messages and the story use common words.
If something gets in your way, please say so; it counts as a bug.

* Check a copy
=INSTALL= says how to check a copy with =sha256sum=.  In short:
#+begin_src sh
sh bin/verify.sh     # is upstream/ Bryant's v1.5, and do the files match SHA256SUMS?
sh bin/test.sh       # every test kit this tree carries
#+end_src

* Check it in a real browser
=tests/browser/topoforge.browser.mjs= opens =index.html= from =file://= in
Chromium, draws a small network, works the panels by keyboard alone, saves
the story, and runs the axe-core accessibility scan in the light and dark
themes.  From 1.7 it also serves =index.html= from a throwaway server on
127.0.0.1 (and a second server on another port, as "the other origin") to
check =?load=<path>=: a CSV and a JSON drawing open from the page's own
origin on a first visit, another origin and =file://= are refused and said,
a 404 is said, and =importCSV= is there.  It needs Node (18 or later),
Playwright and axe-core.  Install the two packages in a scratch folder, or
beside this tree, and run the kit from that folder:
#+begin_src sh
mkdir -p ~/tf-kit && cd ~/tf-kit
npm i playwright axe-core
npx playwright install chromium     # once; skip if you already have one
node /path/to/topoforge/tests/browser/topoforge.browser.mjs /path/to/topoforge/index.html
#+end_src
It looks for =./node_modules/playwright= and
=./node_modules/axe-core/axe.min.js= in the folder you run it from; set
=PW_MODULE=, =AXE= or =CHROMIUM= to point it elsewhere.  The second
argument names a folder for its scans and downloads (default
=out-topoforge/= where you run it; git ignores =out-*/=).

It prints one line per check and writes =tests/browser/RESULT.txt= beside
itself (set =RESULT= to write it elsewhere).  Reading =RESULT.txt=:
- The first line is GREEN (every check passed) or RED (at least one
  failed), computed by the kit, never typed by hand.
- It names the =index.html= it tested by sha256; =bin/ship.sh= refuses to
  pack unless that line matches the =index.html= being shipped and the
  first line is GREEN.
- A check fails on any critical or serious axe finding; moderate and
  minor ones are listed after =::= but do not fail it, except heading
  order, which has its own check.
- Exit 0 green, 1 red, 2 kit broken (Playwright, axe-core or the file
  not found).

For the =?load= checks alone (no axe-core needed):
#+begin_src sh
node /path/to/topoforge/tests/browser/topoforge.browser.mjs --load-only /path/to/topoforge/index.html
sh /path/to/topoforge/tests/browser/prove-red.sh /path/to/topoforge/index.html
#+end_src
The first writes =tests/browser/RESULT-load.txt=, whose first line is
PARTIAL-GREEN or PARTIAL-RED.  It is not =RESULT.txt= (no axe scan, no keyboard
checks were run), and =bin/ship.sh= does not read it.  The second plants three
faults in copies of =index.html= (the origin test, the =file:= test, and the
guard that keeps the sample drawing out of a =?load=); each must turn its own
check, and only that one, red.  Exit 0 every plant was caught, 1 one was not,
2 it could not run.
