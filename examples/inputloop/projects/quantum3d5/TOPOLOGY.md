# quantum3d5

A 3D particle, atom and molecule simulator for learning chemistry and physics in the browser, offline.
Bryant's. Needs a browser and nothing else: no account, no server, 0 network connections as of
2026-10-29. A Realism page says which parts are real physics, simplified, or faked.
