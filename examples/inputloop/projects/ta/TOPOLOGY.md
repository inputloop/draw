# ta

Technology architecture: a portable, host-agnostic operating-model kit for AI help. The shippable is one
file, `AGENTS.md`, identified by the SHA-256 of its bytes (`AGENTS.md.sha256`): drop it at the top of a
new project, or attach it to a session, with a short instruction, and the AI grounds itself in that
host's public identity and prefers the host's own words and standards over its own.

`RECIPE.org` is the front door and the build. The rest is lineage, kept as it was: earlier models,
decision records, papers, a guide of techniques by kind of AI reader with what would show each one
wrong, and the run behind it. It uses public sources only. Corwin's; it works for its makers and has
not yet been reviewed by anyone else. Prose CC BY 4.0, scripts GPL-3.0-or-later, as its README gives them.
