# NextTime-Dev

Community time banking for Nextcloud: post a request, volunteer, earn hours. An hour of help is worth
an hour whoever gives it. An admin, or a three-vote community process, approves hours, and every
movement of hours lands in a public ledger. It is definitelynotaspren's work (Brigidt), tested
on seven Nextcloud versions as of 2026-10-29. A browser demo is in `demo/index.html`.
