# topology-paint

topology paint, the drawing and painting program built into topology: one C program for Windows, Linux
and macOS with brushes, layers, pen pressure and tilt, OpenRaster and Photoshop files, and mods. Made
for Bryant Frankford. `paint/build.bat` or `paint/build.sh` builds, installs and updates it;
`sh bin/test.sh` checks its core.
