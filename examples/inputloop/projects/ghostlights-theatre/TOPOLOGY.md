# ghostlights-theatre

The stage behind ghostlights.theater: a small static site for shows, each made from one piece of writing,
where every claim names its witness (the file it came from, when it was read, a hash of what was read).
The theatre, not the repertory: what someone needs to host their own. Served from gw33. Corwin's.
