# pubs

The reading room: a copy of the publication shelf, where finished writing lands. It holds `papers/`,
`posts/`, `persona/` sheets, the `canon/` and the `books/`. The repository is named `pubs` and the
shelf `pub`; both spellings are meant.

It carries no history of its own beyond refreshes (see the path `pubs-refresh`). A site's tree and the
shelf's working sets are left out on purpose. Corwin's. Code GPL-3.0-or-later OR LGPL-3.0-or-later;
writing CC-BY-SA-4.0, unless an item says otherwise.
